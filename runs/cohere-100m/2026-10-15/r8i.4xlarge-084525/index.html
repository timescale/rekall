Each configuration is measured at its operating point for the target: its fastest sweep point whose recall reaches it. Values are measured there, not interpolated, so the recall can be above the target. Each cell is wall-clock QPS over the whole run, then in parentheses the throughput while every client is busy, which leaves out the drain at the end, and below them the p50 and p95 query latency over all clients. With more than one client, N× is the speedup: wall-clock QPS over the same configuration's single-client wall-clock QPS at that target.
